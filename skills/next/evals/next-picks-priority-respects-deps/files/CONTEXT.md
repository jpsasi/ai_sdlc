# Referral

The shop's referral programme.

## Language

**Credit**:
Money a customer can spend at checkout, earned through referrals.
_Avoid_: points, balance (as a noun for credit itself)

**Ledger entry**:
One record of credit added to, or spent by, a customer.
_Avoid_: transaction

**Referral**:
A friend who signed up through a customer's link.
